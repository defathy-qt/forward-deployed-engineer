## 必问问题 / Required questions

### A. 现状工作流 / As-is workflow
1. 这件事今天从触发到结束，完整经过哪些步骤、经过谁的手？/ Walk me through one real case, step by step and person by person.
2. 哪些步骤要等人、催办、返工？平均/最慢多久？/ Where do you wait, chase, or rework? Average and worst-case time?
3. 工作在哪里离开正式系统（Excel、群消息、私人记录）？/ Where does work leave the official system?

### B. 痛点量化 / Quantified pain
4. 这类事一个月多少量？错误/退回率多少？/ Monthly volume? Error or return rate?
5. 现在投入多少人力？谁是离了就转不动的人？/ How many FTE? Who is the irreplaceable expert?
6. 上一次出问题是什么时候、怎么处理的？/ Last incident and how it was handled?

### C. 既有尝试与约束 / Prior fixes & constraints
7. 以前试过什么办法解决？为什么没成？/ What was tried before and why did it fail?
8. 数据在哪些系统、质量如何、谁能授权访问？/ Which systems hold the data, how clean, who grants access?
9. 有哪些"大家都知道"的规矩、冻结期、审批链？/ Tacit rules, freeze windows, approval chains?

### D. 结果与责任 / Outcome & ownership
10. 如果这件事做到什么程度，你愿意为结果负责并改变团队工作方式？/ What result would make you own the outcome and change how the team works?
11. 什么情况发生就该立刻停？/ What should make us stop immediately?
